Ytics

Ytics is a free, browser-based dashboard builder for data analytics and visualization. Import your data (such as CSV files), build interactive dashboards with a wide range of charts — bar, line, pie, scatter, geographic maps, pivot tables and more — and explore your data with filters and selections. Everything runs entirely in your browser; no account is required to use it. You can optionally save and open your dashboards from Google Drive.

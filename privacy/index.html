Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

ytics ("the App", "we", "us") is a free, browser-based dashboard and data-visualization tool available at https://ytics.online. This policy explains what data the App accesses, how it is used, and how it is protected.

In short: ytics runs entirely in your browser. We do not operate servers that collect, store, or transmit your data. We do not sell or share your data with anyone. The App only accesses the specific Google Drive files you explicitly choose to open or that the App creates for you.

1. Data You Provide

ytics lets you import data files (such as CSV files) and build dashboards from them. All of this data is processed locally in your browser. Dashboards and settings you create are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. This information never leaves your device except when you explicitly choose to save a file to Google Drive (see below).

2. Google User Data & Google Drive Access

ytics offers an optional feature to open and save dashboard files (.ytics files) directly to your Google Drive. To provide this, the App requests a single, narrowly-scoped permission:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — this scope grants the App access only to the individual files you explicitly open through the Google File Picker or that the App itself creates. The App cannot see, read, or access any other files in your Google Drive.

We use this access solely to let you load an existing dashboard file and to save your dashboards back to your Drive. The file content is transferred directly between your browser and Google's servers. We do not route it through, copy it to, or retain it on any server of ours — because we do not operate any such server.

3. Limited Use Disclosure

ytics's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically:

  • We only use Google user data to provide and improve the features described in this policy.
  • We do not transfer or sell Google user data to third parties, advertising platforms, data brokers, or for any purpose unrelated to the App's features.
  • We do not use Google user data for advertising.
  • We do not allow humans to read your Google user data unless you give explicit consent, it is necessary for security purposes (e.g. investigating abuse), or required by law.

4. Data Storage & Retention

Your dashboards and preferences are stored locally in your browser (local storage) and remain under your control. You can clear them at any time through your browser settings. Files you save to Google Drive are stored in your own Google account and are governed by Google's Privacy Policy. Because we do not operate servers, we do not retain any copy of your data.

5. Third-Party Services

The App is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google's authentication and Drive APIs to provide the optional Drive integration. Your interactions with these providers are subject to their respective privacy policies.

6. Cookies & Tracking

ytics does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies. Any browser storage used is strictly for the App's functionality (such as remembering your dashboards and settings).

7. Children's Privacy

ytics is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Revoking Access

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, contact the developer at candiolof@gmail.com.

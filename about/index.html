Ytics

A free, browser-based dashboard builder for data analytics and visualization.

What is Ytics?

Ytics is a web application that lets anyone turn raw data into interactive dashboards — no installation, no account, and no cost. You import your own data (such as CSV files), design dashboards on a drag-and-drop grid, and explore the results with filters and cross-chart selections. Everything runs entirely in your browser; your data stays on your device.

What you can do with it

  • Import and transform your own datasets (CSV and more).
  • Build dashboards with dozens of chart types — bar, line, pie, scatter, geographic maps, pivot tables, heat maps, treemaps, and many others.
  • Filter and drill into your data with interactive selections that update every chart at once.
  • Define calculated measures and aggregations without writing code.
  • Optionally open and save your dashboard files to your own Google Drive.

Google Drive integration

Ytics offers an optional feature to open and save dashboard files directly to your Google Drive. It requests only the narrow drive.file permission, which grants access solely to the files you explicitly open or that Ytics creates — never the rest of your Drive. See our Privacy Policy for details.
